Claude, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: Claude's design system ("cds") colour ramps, the semantic palette it declares for light on .cds-root and again under data-mode="dark", its type roles and density scale, the sidebar frame's own sizes, and the layout numbers measured on the page. The toggle in the header sets data-mode on <html>, which is how Claude switches. The drawings below are rebuilt from the tokens; the real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from claude.ai on 2026-10-04, signed in, through static copies of each page. Anthropic Sans, Serif and Mono are Anthropic's own faces and are not loaded here: the sans falls back to the system face, the serif to Source Serif 4 as a stand-in, the mono to ui-monospace.

Colour

Claude names colours by role. surface is the ground (0 under everything, 1 the app, 2 raised, 3 the composer and menus), text is primary, secondary, muted and disabled, fill is buttons and the ghost hover every row uses, alpha is the ink at fixed opacities that hairlines and hovers are made of, border is two of those. The neutral scale flips in dark. Each swatch shows the active theme's value; flip the header toggle to compare.

Ramps

Nine hues of 35 steps each, from 0 (white) to 900 (#0b0b0b), declared once and shared by both themes. The gray carries a faint warm cast; clay (#d97757) and clay-emphasized (#c6613f) are the brand pigments outside the ramps.

Typography

A sans for the interface, a serif for the voice: the wordmark, the greeting, page titles and, by default, Claude's own replies. The scale is seven roles (caption, footnote, code, body, prose, heading, title) at four weights; semibold is 580 and bold 600. These are the comfortable sizes the account uses; compact is one step smaller.

Density, space and shape

Claude sizes its controls through the --cds-* density scale rather than a plain spacing scale: radius, control heights, icon size, five paddings and five gaps. The value is the comfortable density; the faded number after it is compact, which Claude falls back to when no density is set and forces on some dense components.

Elevation

Rings, not borders. A 1px ring of the 10% ink is drawn as a box-shadow: outset in light (--cds-ring-outer) and inset in dark (--cds-ring-inner), with a soft drop under menus and the composer.

Motion

Fast and springy: buttons press in 60 ms, most UI answers in 120 ms on a snap curve, sheets take 300 ms. Click a track to play it on its own curve.

Layout

A 288 px sidebar on its own slightly darker ground, then the content column 8 px further on. Pages hang from a 48 px header: the home centres a 672 px composer under the greeting, a chat a 768 px transcript, and list pages a 960 px column.
