# Coursera public pages: URLs and measured layout

All pages were captured on 2026-09-29, signed out, with headless Chromium 1.63 (Chrome 140 user agent), no cookies, no sign-in, no enrolment and no form submitted. Desktop is 1440x900 at 2x, mobile is 390x844 at 2x with a touch Android user agent. Geometry below was read with `getBoundingClientRect()` by `tools/capture/layout.mjs` (plus a few one-off probes of the same kind), with the AI chat panel closed unless a row says otherwise. Numbers are CSS pixels. Page heights move by a few hundred pixels between loads because carousels, promo banners and lazy images differ, so treat them as approximate.

## Shared frame

| Thing | Measured |
|---|---|

A 40px black audience strip (For Individuals, For Businesses, For Universities, For Governments, active one underlined in white) over a 65px nav: wordmark, Explore menu button, Degrees, a pill search field about 475px wide with a round blue search button, the AI sparkle button, Log In, Join for Free (outlined). |
| Content container | 1440px max width, centred, 48px side padding on course, certificate, specialization and browse pages (1344px inner). Search and home use 32px. At a 1920 viewport the container is x 240 to 1680 and the header still spans the full width. |
| Type | Source Sans Pro. Body 16/24, page h1 600 44/52 on course pages. |
| Footer | Full width, about 1745px tall on course pages: column headings (Skills, Professional Certificates, Courses & Specializations, Career Resources), then Coursera, Community and More with app store badges, social icons and the copyright line. The link lists under the headings did not render in headless captures, so the footer shots show headings over empty space. |
| Cookie banner | OneTrust is in the DOM but no banner showed in a fresh headless context, so nothing needed declining. `#onetrust-consent-sdk` is hidden by `public.mjs` anyway. |
| AI panel (desktop only) | Fixed `aside`, 416px wide by 795px tall, x 1024 to 1440, y 105 to 900, on course, specialization, certificate and course2 pages. It opens by default a few seconds after load and pushes the content into the remaining 1024px, which makes the page 300 to 400px taller. Contents: sparkle icon and a close X, a blue privacy notice card with a Got it button, the purple gradient heading "Is this right for me?", a paragraph, three suggestion chips, an "Ask anything..." field with a send button, a disclaimer. Absent on home, search, browse and on every mobile page (mobile shows an "Is this right for me?" chip in the hero instead). |
| Dark | A forced token flip, not a shipped theme: Coursera has no dark setting and ignores `prefers-color-scheme` (a dark-scheme context still loads with `data-color-theme="light"`). Setting `document.documentElement.dataset.colorTheme = 'dark'` and `style.colorScheme = 'dark'` after load re-renders every page coherently, because each colour token is a `light-dark()` pair. Quirks: partner logos and some art keep their light look (logos gain a white pill), the audience strip is black in both, and the tab bar overflow fade stays light. |

## Mobile (390x844)

Header is 65px, `position: sticky; top: 0`, with a hamburger, the wordmark and a search icon (the audience strip and search field are gone). Side gutter is 16px on course, specialization and home (h1 at x 16, 358px wide), 12px on search where the result cards are a single column 366px wide. On course-like pages a bar is fixed to the bottom of the viewport, full width and 92px tall (y 752 to 844) holding the Enroll for free button. There is no AI panel; an "Is this right for me?" chip sits under the enrol button instead. The stat card becomes a stacked list (3 modules, 4.9, Beginner level, Flexible schedule, 98%), there is no in-page tab bar, and the weeks or courses are rows with a chevron that open a full-height sheet (title, a close X, the content) instead of expanding in place. In the full-page shots the fixed CTA bar shows up mid-page, where the tile boundary or scroll happened to leave it; ignore it. Full-page heights: home about 8500, search about 12100, course about 11100 to 11500, specialization about 11300.
